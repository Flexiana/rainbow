What is established, and at which level

Rainbow grades every claim on its maturity scale (doc/maturity-levels.md): L3 is a kernel-checked theorem about a mathematical model, L4 is the kernel checking one particular output against such a theorem, L5 adds a proved link to a printed program, and no artifact in the repository is L6. The levels below are the ones the repository records.
